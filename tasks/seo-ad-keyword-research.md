# SEO & Ad Campaign Keyword Research
*Research completed: 2026-02-21*

---

## TL;DR — What You Need to Know

**The market is real and growing:** Pet memorial products alone = ~$500M market growing at 7% CAGR. Personalized gifts overall = $30B globally. You're in a sweet spot.

**Nobody does what you do.** Every competitor's "personalization" means stamping a name on a generic poem. The closest competitor to your custom-poem concept (The Gifted Rhyme) charges $90+ for the poem alone, takes 2 weeks, and doesn't include framing. You do it instantly with a preview for $84.95 including museum-quality framing.

**The two buyers you need to reach:**
1. **The grieving person** (self-purchase) — searches "pet memorial," "in memory of my dog"
2. **The gift-giver** (buying for someone else) — searches "gift for someone who lost a dog," "pet sympathy gift"

**Best ad channels:** Google Search/Shopping (highest intent) + Facebook/Instagram (best discovery + lowest CPC in pet niche).

### Facebook Targeting Strategy

**Interest-based (Top of Funnel):**
- Pet owners (dogs, cats — target separately)
- Specific breed interest groups
- Pet adoption/rescue interests
- Veterinary interests
- Rainbow Bridge (concept)
- Pet photography
- ASPCA, Humane Society followers
- Pet insurance (signals high emotional investment)

**Retargeting (Mid/Bottom Funnel):**
- Website visitors (30 days)
- Product page viewers who didn't purchase
- Cart abandoners (14 days)
- Past purchasers (for gift-for-others messaging)

**Lookalikes (Scale Phase):**
- 1% lookalike of purchasers (your #1 audience once you have 100+ sales)
- 1% lookalike of add-to-cart users
- Scale to 2-3% as budget grows

### Creative Format Rankings

| Format | Best For | Performance |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| Carousel | Multiple styles/examples | Best conversion rate, lowest CAC |
| Video (15-30 sec) | Process reveals, unboxings | Highest CTR, best for awareness |
| UGC/Testimonial | Customer stories | Highest trust, best for mid-funnel |
| Single Image | Clean product shots | Solid baseline, good for retargeting |

### Ad Copy Frameworks

**Critical Rule: Lead with love, not grief. Never exploit pain.**

**Angle 1 — The "Forever" Hook:**
> "Flowers last a week. This stays on the wall forever."
> Custom memorial portrait with a poem written from your memories. Museum-quality framing included.

**Angle 2 — The Gift-Giver:**
> "Your friend just lost their dog and you have no idea what to send. Don't send flowers."
> A personalized poem that mentions the real memories — the way they stole socks, the spot on the couch, the look in their eyes.

**Angle 3 — The Specificity Sell:**
> "Most 'personalized' pet memorials just add a name to a generic poem. Ours mentions the way they knocked things off the counter at 3am."

**Angle 4 — The Anti-Generic:**
> "Everyone sent flowers. You sent the gift that made the whole room cry."

**Angle 5 — The Quality Story:**
> "Not a print. A portrait. Hand-finished, archival paper, museum-quality frame. Made to stay on your wall for 100 years."

**Angle 6 — The Brand Name IS the Headline:**
> "Still Beside Me."
> A personalized memorial poem and portrait. Because some bonds are forever.

**Headlines to AVOID (exploitative / policy violations):**
- "Dealing with pet loss?"
- "Grieving your pet?"
- "Lost your best friend?"
