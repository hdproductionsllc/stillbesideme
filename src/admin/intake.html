Etsy order intake

All orders

For a sale that came in through Etsy. Copy what the buyer answered at checkout, add their photo, and this creates a real order in awaiting review, exactly like one from our own site. Nothing is written, proofed or printed here: the next stop is the normal review page, and nothing reaches the printer until you record the buyer approving their proof in Etsy Messages.
