What is a Letter From Heaven?

It's a first-person letter from the person you lost to the people they love – written from real memories, in their voice, as if they had one more chance to say everything they'd want you to know.

Written in their voice

This isn't a generic poem with their name inserted. The letter captures how they actually spoke – their phrases, their humor, their way of showing love. A grandmother who said "bless your heart" sounds nothing like a father who called everyone "kiddo."

Mentions real memories

The letter references the details that made your relationship yours – Sunday morning routines, the recipe that was never written down, the way they answered the phone. Not generic sentiments. The real things only your family knows.

A permanent display piece

Printed on archival paper, set alongside their photo in a handcrafted museum-quality frame. It becomes the thing families read every morning, the piece visitors stop and ask about, the in loving memory gift that lasts forever.

Questions about Letter From Heaven

What is a Letter From Heaven?

A Letter From Heaven is a personalized first-person letter written from the perspective of someone who passed away. It's written as if they're speaking directly to the people they love – mentioning real memories, personality traits, and details only your family would know. The letter is printed alongside their photo in a museum-quality handcrafted frame. It's not a generic poem – it's a memorial gift that lasts because it's built from the real details of their life.

How does AI create a personal letter?

You share memories about the person – their personality, things they used to say, how they made people feel. Our AI uses those details to draft a first-person letter in their voice. You review every word, edit anything you want, and approve the final version before it goes to print. Nothing is generic – it's built entirely from what you share.

Will it really sound like them?

Yes. We ask about how they spoke, phrases they used, and the way they showed love. A grandmother who said "bless your heart" sounds completely different from a father who called everyone "kiddo." The more details you share, the more it sounds like them. Families regularly tell us it feels like hearing from them one more time.

Can I edit the letter before it prints?

Absolutely. You see a full design proof before anything goes to print. Edit every word, regenerate the letter, or start over. Nothing prints until you say it's right. 100% satisfaction guarantee.

What relationships does this work for?

Letter From Heaven works for any relationship. We have dedicated pages for loss of a mother, father, spouse, grandparent, sibling, child, and close friend. The letter is written from their perspective, tailored to the specific relationship and the memories you share. You can also send it as a sympathy gift instead of flowers.

How is this different from a generic memorial poem?

Generic memorial poems use the same words for everyone. A Letter From Heaven is written specifically about the person you lost – their name, their personality, the things they said, the memories you shared. It's a first-person letter in their voice, not a one-size-fits-all verse. That's what makes it the memorial gift families tell us becomes the most meaningful thing in their home.
