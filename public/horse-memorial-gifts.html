Copper
1994 – 2023
Dear Copper,

You knew my step on the gravel
long before I reached the gate.
Twenty nine winters of that sound,
and you never once got it wrong.

You put your head on my shoulder
and the whole day went quiet.
I am still the person
you made of me at fifteen.
"Big Red"
Beloved partner of the Halvorsen family

Horse people understand the size of this.

Thirty years will not fit on a plaque

A horse is not one chapter of your life. You learned to drive so you could get to the barn. The poem is written from what you actually remember: the corner of the field he picked out of forty acres, the way he swung his head when he heard your car on the lane. It sounds like him because every detail in it is his.

His name belongs on a wall

A halter on a hook in the tack room means everything to you and nothing to anyone who walks past it. This carries his name, his years, his photograph and his poem, hung in the hallway or over the desk in the barn office, the same as any other member of the family.

For everyone who never met him

Most of the people in your life never stood next to him. They do not know what two decades of twice a day looks like, and they will not understand why you took the week off. Grief that happens at a barn instead of at home is harder to explain and harder to be seen in. A tribute on the wall says it for you.

Questions about horse memorial gifts

What photo works if most of mine were taken from across the arena?

More of them will work than you expect. A phone photo is fine. What matters is that his head and his eye are in focus and take up a decent part of the picture. Barn light is dim and yellow almost everywhere, and that is normal to work with. Send the photo you love: you see the finished design before you pay, and if it will not print well at the size you chose, we tell you first.

Can the design use his barn name and his show name?

Yes. Most horses answer to one name and are registered under another. You can set the barn name large with the show name beneath it, or the other way around, with the years alongside. Tell us which name the poem should use and that is the one it uses.

He was a lease horse and never legally mine. Can I still do this?

Yes. Whose name was on the paperwork has very little to do with who did the mucking and the wrapping and the loving. There is no ownership requirement of any kind: a school horse, a horse you rode at the barn for ten years, or one you leased for a season all count.

Which size suits a tack room, and which suits the house?

The 11x14 at $119 is the size most people choose, and it holds a longer poem comfortably at reading distance. The 8x10 at $79 suits a tack room shelf or a narrow strip of wall. Over a mantel or up a stairway, the 16x20 at $169 and the 20x30 at $249 give the poem real presence. There is also a print only 11x14 at $39 if you would rather frame it yourself, and a digital file at $19.95.

Can the photograph have both of us in it, horse and rider?

Yes, and many people choose exactly that. A picture of the two of you at the gate, or walking out after a class, often says what a posed conformation shot cannot. The poem can be written to him, or written to you about him. Tell us which sounds right and we write it that way.

How long does it take to arrive?

Roughly 8 to 12 business days from the day you approve the design to the day it reaches your door, with free shipping within the US. Nothing starts until you have read the poem and said yes.

What if the words are not right?

Tell us and we change them. You read the poem and see the whole design before you pay, and you can take a full refund any time before it goes to print. Nobody should hang a tribute that sounds like somebody else’s horse.
