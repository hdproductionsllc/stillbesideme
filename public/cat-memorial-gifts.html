Questions about cat memorial gifts

What makes this different from other cat memorial gifts?

Most cat memorials are a paw print or a generic rainbow bridge ornament. Ours is a personalized poem written from the memories you share – their personality, their quirks, the things that made them yours. Printed alongside their photo in an elegant, museum-quality frame.

Does the poem mention things specific to my cat?

Yes. If your cat knocked things off the counter, claimed a particular sunbeam, or had a specific way of demanding attention, those details go into the poem. People read it and say "that's exactly them."

What if my cat was hard to photograph?

We understand – cats aren't always cooperative subjects. A clear phone photo is all you need, and before printing a real person reviews it. If it won't print well at the size you chose, we'll reach out before anything is made.

Can I send this to someone who just lost their cat?

Absolutely. People often underestimate how much someone grieves a cat. This gift shows you understand. Even just a name and photo make a beautiful tribute, and we can ship directly to the recipient.

Can I edit the poem before it's printed?

Yes. You see a full preview with the photo, poem, and frame before anything goes to print. Edit any line, regenerate the poem, or choose from our curated library. You approve every word before it prints.

Do you make memorial gifts for dogs and other pets too?

Yes. We create personalized memorial tributes for dogs, horses, rabbits, birds, and any companion animal. Visit our Pet Memorial Gifts page or our Dog Memorial Gifts page for more.

What happens to the memories I share about my cat?

They’re kept, not discarded. Every order includes a private page with your cat’s photo, their poem, and the memories you wrote – a quiet place to return to whenever you miss them.
