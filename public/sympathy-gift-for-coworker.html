Questions about sending a sympathy gift to a coworker

Is a personalized memorial gift appropriate from a coworker?

Absolutely. A personalized tribute shows genuine care without crossing professional boundaries. Many of our orders come from coworkers and work teams who want to send something more meaningful than a card or flowers.

Can we send this as a group gift from the whole office?

Yes. Many teams pool together for a single meaningful gift. You can include all names in the card message at checkout. One person handles the order and everyone contributes.

I don't know much about their loved one. Can I still order?

Yes. Even just a name and a photo make a beautiful tribute. We guide you through the process and the poem is personalized from whatever details you can share. You'd be surprised how much a few small details can create.

Can you ship it directly to my coworker?

Yes. Enter their home address at checkout and we'll ship it straight to their door, beautifully packaged and ready to hang. No awkward office delivery – it arrives at home where it belongs.

What if I'm not sure whether they lost a person or a pet?

We have collections for both. Our Pet Memorial collection is for pet loss, and our Letter From Heaven collection is for human loss – parents, spouses, grandparents, siblings, and friends.

How long does it take to arrive?

Most orders ship within 5–7 business days. Rush options are available at checkout for faster delivery. Every frame is carefully packaged to arrive in perfect condition.
