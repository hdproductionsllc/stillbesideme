Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 12, 2026

Still Beside Me is operated by HD Productions LLC, a company based in the United States. We create personalized memorial wall art — poems, letters, and tributes that honor the people and pets you’ve loved and lost.

We understand that the information you share with us is deeply personal. The names, dates, memories, and photos you entrust to us represent some of the most meaningful parts of your life. We treat that trust with the care and respect it deserves. This privacy policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

When you place an order or interact with our website, we may collect the following types of information:

Personal details you provide: Your name, email address, shipping address, and phone number (if provided). These are necessary to fulfill and deliver your order.

Memories and stories about your loved one: The names, dates, relationships, and personal memories you share with us during the ordering process. These details are used to create your personalized poem or letter.

Photos: The photographs you upload of your loved one or pet. These are used solely to create your memorial tribute.

Payment information: Credit card and billing details are collected during checkout. These are processed securely by Stripe and are never stored on our servers (see the Payment Information section below for more details).

Automatically collected information: When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring website. This is collected through cookies and analytics tools to help us understand how people use our site and to improve your experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for the following purposes:

Order fulfillment: Your personal details, memories, and photos are used to create your personalized memorial tribute, process your payment, print and frame your piece, and ship it to you.

AI-powered poem and letter generation: The memories and details you share are processed by our AI system to generate a personalized poem or letter for your tribute. See the section below for a full explanation of how this works.

Communication: We use your email address to send order confirmations, shipping updates, proof approvals, and to respond to any questions you have. We may also send you a follow-up email after delivery to make sure you’re happy with your order.

Website improvement: We use analytics data to understand how visitors use our website so we can make it better, faster, and easier to use.

Marketing (only with your permission): If you opt in to our mailing list, we may send you occasional emails about new products, seasonal offerings, or helpful content related to grief and remembrance.

AI-Generated Content

One of the things that makes Still Beside Me special is that every poem and letter we create is unique to your loved one. Here’s how that works, and what it means for your privacy:

When you place an order, the memories, names, dates, and details you share are sent to Anthropic’s Claude AI, which generates a personalized poem or letter based on what you’ve told us. This is not a generic, fill-in-the-blank template — it’s a genuinely original piece of writing crafted from the real moments and qualities you described.

What you should know:

Your memories are sent to Anthropic’s API solely for the purpose of generating your poem or letter. Anthropic does not use your data to train their AI models. The information is processed and the poem is returned to us — that’s it. We do not share your memories with Anthropic for any other purpose, and Anthropic’s data handling practices are governed by their own privacy policy and API terms of service. You can review Anthropic’s privacy practices at anthropic.com/privacy.

We want you to feel completely comfortable sharing your memories with us. The stories you tell us about your loved one are sacred, and they are used for one purpose only: to create something beautiful in their honor.

Photos and Personal Memories

The photos you upload and the memories you share are among the most sensitive information we handle. Here is how we treat them:

Storage: Your uploaded photos are stored securely on our servers for the purpose of fulfilling your order. They are used to create the final printed tribute that is shipped to you.

Access: Only authorized members of our team access your photos and memories, and only as needed to complete your order — for example, to prepare your print file or to coordinate with our printing partner.

Sharing: Your photos and the personal details of your memories are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. The only third party that receives your photo is our professional printing partner, Luma Prints, who prints and frames your tribute. They receive only what is necessary to produce your order.

No public display: We will never post your photo or your loved one’s story publicly (on our website, social media, or anywhere else) without your explicit, written permission.

Payment Information

All payments on Still Beside Me are processed by Stripe, a leading and trusted payment processor. When you enter your credit or debit card information at checkout, that data goes directly to Stripe’s secure servers.

We never see, handle, or store your full card number. Stripe provides us only with a summary — such as the last four digits of your card and the transaction amount — so we can reference your order. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant, which is the highest standard of security in the payment industry.

For more information on how Stripe handles your payment data, you can visit their privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to provide a smooth experience and to help us understand how our site is being used.

Session cookies: We use session cookies to keep you logged in during your visit and to remember the items in your order as you move through our site. These cookies expire after 30 days of inactivity.

Google Analytics (GA4): We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized data about how visitors interact with our website — things like which pages are most popular, how long people spend on the site, and where our traffic comes from. This helps us improve the website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. Google’s privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Meta Pixel: We may use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram. This pixel may collect data about your browsing activity on our site, which Meta uses to help us show relevant ads. You can learn more about Meta’s data practices and manage your ad preferences at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies, though this may affect your experience on our site.

Marketing Emails

We use Mailchimp to manage our email mailing list. We will only add you to our marketing email list if you explicitly opt in — for example, by checking a box during checkout or signing up through a form on our website.

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any marketing email. We will never send you marketing emails without your consent, and we will never sell or share your email address with other companies for their marketing purposes.

Order-related emails (confirmations, shipping updates, proof approvals) are transactional, not marketing. You will receive these regardless of your marketing preferences because they are necessary to fulfill your order.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information, order details, photos, and generated poems for as long as necessary to fulfill your order and provide customer support. Specifically:

Order records (name, email, shipping address, order details, generated poems) are retained indefinitely so that we can assist you with reorders, replacements, or questions about past orders.

Uploaded photos are retained on our servers to support any follow-up requests, such as reprints or modifications. If you would like your photos deleted after your order is complete, simply contact us and we will remove them.

Analytics data is retained according to the default retention settings of Google Analytics and Meta, which are governed by their respective privacy policies.

If you request deletion of your data (see Your Rights below), we will remove your personal information from our active systems within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain records (such as financial transaction records for tax and accounting purposes).

Third-Party Services

We work with a small number of trusted third-party services to operate our business. Each of these services receives only the minimum information necessary for them to perform their function:

Stripe — Processes payments securely. Receives your billing details and payment card information. (Privacy Policy)

Anthropic (Claude AI) — Generates personalized poems and letters. Receives the memories, names, and details you provide during the ordering process. (Privacy Policy)

Google Analytics (GA4) — Provides anonymized website usage analytics. Receives browsing data through cookies. (Privacy Policy)

Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — Measures advertising effectiveness. May receive browsing data through the Meta Pixel. (Privacy Policy)

Luma Prints — Our professional printing and framing partner. Receives your photo and the final tribute design file for production, as well as your shipping address for delivery.

Mailchimp — Manages our marketing email list. Receives your email address and name if you opt in to marketing emails. (Privacy Policy)

Nodemailer / Resend — Sends transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates, proof approvals). Receives your email address and order-related information necessary to generate those emails.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party, ever.

Your Rights

We believe you should have control over your personal information. You have the right to:

Access your data: You can request a copy of the personal information we have on file for you, including your order history, uploaded photos, and any stored memories.

Correct your data: If any information we have is inaccurate, let us know and we will update it.

Delete your data: You can request that we delete your personal information, photos, and memories from our systems. We will honor this request within 30 days, with the exception of records we are legally required to retain (such as financial transaction records).

Opt out of marketing: You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email, or by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at hello@stillbesideme.com. We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at hello@stillbesideme.com and we will promptly delete that information.

We recognize that many of our customers are honoring children who have passed away. The memories, names, and photos of deceased children that are shared by adult customers for the purpose of creating a memorial tribute are handled with the same care and respect as all other order information described in this policy.

Security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS), secure server infrastructure, and limited access controls so that only authorized team members can view your information.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. For significant changes, we may also notify you by email.
