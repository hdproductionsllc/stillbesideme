2. Our Products and Services

Still Beside Me creates personalized memorial wall art. Each piece is a one-of-a-kind tribute that combines:

  • An AI-generated poem or letter, written from the memories, stories, and feelings you share with us
  • A photo of your loved one (human or pet) that you provide

Every product is custom-made to order. No two pieces are the same, because no two relationships are the same.

3. AI-Generated Content

The poems and letters in our products are generated by artificial intelligence, guided by the memories, personality details, and sentiments you provide during the ordering process. Here is how it works:

  • You share memories. You tell us about your loved one — their personality, your favorite moments together, the things you want to say.
  • AI creates a draft. Our AI uses what you have shared to compose a personalized poem or letter that captures your unique relationship.
  • You review and approve. You will receive a design proof that includes the full poem or letter. Nothing goes to print until you have reviewed it and given your approval.

The generated poem or letter becomes part of your purchased product upon approval. While AI helps us craft these words, the heart behind every piece comes from you.
