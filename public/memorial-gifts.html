Questions you might have

How does this work?

You share memories of your pet – their personality, their quirks, the way they greeted you at the door. We write a personalized poem from those memories, printed beside their photo. You'll see the proof within 24 hours and approve every word before we print.

What makes this different from other pet memorial gifts?

Most pet memorials are a name stamped on a generic product. This is a poem written from your actual memories, printed beside their photo, with their name and the years you shared. No two are alike.

Does this work for any pet?

Yes. Dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, horses – any companion you loved. The poem is written from the memories you share, tailored to who they were and the life you had together.

Will the poem actually feel like them?

We ask about their personality, their habits, and the little rituals you shared. A dog who stole socks reads differently from a cat who claimed the sunny windowsill. The more details you share, the more it feels like them.

What if I'm not happy with the poem?

You see a full design proof before we print. Edit every word, regenerate the poem, or start over. If the finished piece doesn't meet your expectations, we'll make it right. 100% satisfaction guarantee.

Can I order this as a gift for someone else?

Yes. Many of our orders are gifts for someone who's grieving. Share what you know – even just a name and photo – and we'll create something meaningful. We can ship it directly to the recipient.
