Why this exists

When I lost Littles – my Siamese, my shadow, the one who followed me from room to room and yelled at me until I picked him up – people sent flowers. They sent cards. Every single one was gone within a week. Not because they didn't care, but because there was nothing to keep.

I wanted something that captured who he actually was. Not just his face, but the weird little voice he made when he wanted dinner. The way he'd curl up on my chest every single night. The nickname only we used. The specific, irreplaceable things that made him ours.

I couldn't find it anywhere. So I built it.

Every tribute we make starts with details only someone who loved them would know. The poem is written from those details. Then we print it alongside their photo in a frame that belongs on a wall for the rest of someone's life.

– Rebecca, founder of Still Beside Me

The writing

We don't write about grief. We write the pet back into the room.

Anyone can frame a photo. The poem is the hard part. It puts down the small ordinary things that made them who they were: the one chair, the flat tennis ball, the six o'clock alarm.

Recognizable, not interchangeable.

Most memorial writing stays general. The loyal friend, the hole in the heart, the bridge. Ours goes the other way. It reaches for the one flat grey tennis ball, the truck heard three streets away, the exact spot by the door. A stranger could pick your dog out of a crowd from the poem alone.

The line that undoes you is the one still in the room.

The hardest lines aren't about dying. They're about the ordinary thing left behind, still sitting there, still waiting, as if nothing had changed.

The blue chair is still yours. We just sit beside it now.Pixie
The ball's still on the porch. We couldn't bring ourselves to move it.Rusty
I still wake at six. I still reach across the pillow.Marbles
The house is bigger without you.Callie
Slow down now, sweet one. We will catch up to you.Biscuit

First, it makes you smile.

Before the turn, there's almost always a small joke. The pet caught being exactly, ridiculously themselves. That's what makes them feel alive on the page, so the ache that follows lands on someone real instead of a symbol.

“Head on the pillow like you paid the mortgage.”Marbles
“…and found it, on the whole, acceptable.”Tigger
“Turned up at the door at six on the dot. Dinner, right on time.”Ziggy

A wall of them. No two could be swapped.

Every tribute is written from scratch for one animal, with its own habits, its own room, its own small joke. Hang a dozen together and the writing never repeats itself.

Questions you might have

What is a pet memorial tribute?

A Still Beside Me pet memorial tribute is a framed archival print that pairs your pet's photo with a poem written from your own memories of them – their name and the years you shared set into the design. Four sizes from $79 to $249. You approve every word before it prints, and it arrives framed in about 8-12 business days after you approve your proof, with free US shipping.

Can I edit the poem?

Yes. You see a full preview before we print anything. You can edit every word, regenerate it, or choose from our curated library instead. You're in complete control.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

You see a full design proof before we print. If the finished piece doesn't meet your expectations, we'll make it right. 100% satisfaction guarantee, no fine print.

What makes each tribute personal?

Every poem is written from your own memories of them – their quirks, their personality, the things only your family would know. Their name and the years you shared are set into the design, printed beside a photo you love, all inside an elegant frame you choose. No two are alike, because no two of them were.

How long does shipping take?

Your poem proof arrives within 24 hours. After you approve it, every piece is made to order and arrives in about 8–12 business days. Shipping is free, and we carefully package every frame to arrive in perfect condition.

I'm ordering as a gift and don't know all the details.

That's completely fine. Even just a name and photo make a beautiful tribute. The more you share, the more personal it gets – but we'll guide you through it either way.

What if I don't have a great photo?

A clear phone photo works beautifully. Before printing, a real person reviews the photo you send, and if it's too small or blurry to print well, we'll email you before anything is made.

What sizes are available?

Four sizes: 8×10 at $79, 11×14 at $119 (our most popular), 16×20 at $169, and 20×30 at $249. Every size is an archival, museum-quality print that arrives framed, ready to hang, with free shipping.

What happens to the memories I share?

They're kept. Every order includes a private page – just for you – with their photo, their poem, and the memories you wrote. You can return to it whenever you need them close, and it doesn't expire.
