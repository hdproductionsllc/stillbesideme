Questions you might have

What makes this different from what I see on Amazon?

Most pet memorials stamp a name on a mass-produced product. This is a poem built from the specific memories you share – their quirks, their habits, the things only you would know. Printed alongside their photo in an elegant, museum-quality frame.

Does this work for cats too? Or just dogs?

Any pet. Dogs, cats, horses, rabbits, birds – the poem is written from your memories, so it's personal regardless of species.

What if I only have a phone photo?

That's completely fine. We color-correct, upscale, and optimize every image for print. Even a casual phone photo comes out sharp and beautiful on the finished piece.

Can I change the poem before it's printed?

Yes. You see a full preview before we print anything. Edit every word, regenerate it, or choose from our curated library. You approve the final version before it goes to print.

I want to send this as a gift. Do I need to know everything?

Not at all. Even just a name and photo make a beautiful tribute. The more you share, the more personal it gets – but we'll guide you through it either way. We can ship directly to the recipient.

How long does shipping take?

You receive your poem proof within 24 hours. Each piece is made to order and arrives in 7–10 days, carefully packaged to arrive in perfect condition.
